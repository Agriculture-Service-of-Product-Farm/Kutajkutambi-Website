What it is

Smallholder farmers in India face rapid disease outbreaks, nutrient stress, and limited access to timely agronomic advice. Field conditions include variable lighting, dew, mixed healthy/diseased leaves, and local crop varieties.

Kutajkutambi is the multi-agent AI layer that processes smartphone or drone images, fuses them with sensor and satellite data when available, and returns a clear diagnosis plus treatment plan in the farmer’s language.

Perceive

Leaf segmentation and multi-class disease / pest / nutrient visual detection with Grad-CAM explainability.

Fuse

Combine image results with soil NPK, moisture, weather favorability, and NDVI trends.

Advise

Ranked cultural, biological, and chemical options with dosage, timing, safety, and expected yield impact — Hindi voice or WhatsApp ready.

Not a black box. Every diagnosis includes a confidence score, visual evidence, and a one-tap path to a human ASPF agronomist.

Engineering quality

  • Input validation and graceful handling of blurry or out-of-distribution images
  • Typed agent interfaces and auditable reasoning trails
  • Automated tests for edge cases (lighting, occlusion, mixed symptoms)
  • Model versioning, experiment tracking, and controlled promotion of champion models
  • No external telemetry from production inference packages

Agricultural applications

Early disease scouting

Detect blast, rusts, blights, leaf curl and other common issues before they spread across the plot.

Nutrient & water management

Visual deficiency symptoms fused with sensor NPK and moisture for precise recommendations.

Pest IPM support

Identify beneficials versus pests and estimate density to guide spot treatment.

Seasonal planning

Growth-stage estimation and multi-season rotation / organic transition calendars.
